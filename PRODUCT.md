# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

UniversoVet primarily speaks to pet owners and guardians in Coquimbo who need trustworthy, convenient veterinary care and want a low-friction way to book through WhatsApp. Visitors are likely comparing local options, checking services, confirming location and hours, or looking for preventive care programs for dogs and cats.

## Product Purpose

The site presents UniversoVet as a warm, professional veterinary clinic in Coquimbo and turns visits into WhatsApp conversations for appointments, services, grooming, preventive plans, and seasonal campaigns. Success means a visitor quickly understands the clinic's care style, sees the service they need, trusts the team enough to reach out, and can get location or schedule information without friction.

## Scope

This context is oriented primarily to public brand and conversion surfaces for pet guardians. `admin-bts` is an internal management surface and should not automatically follow the same public landing-page context.

## Positioning

UniversoVet is the approachable, prevention-minded veterinary clinic that treats each pet with clinical responsibility and family-level warmth.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: book or ask for an appointment through WhatsApp.
- Secondary CTA: browse services, preventive plans, campaigns, location, hours, and coupon lookup flows before contacting the clinic.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: thoughtful veterinary care for every stage of a pet's life, close to home in Coquimbo.
- Belief ladder: UniversoVet is local and reachable; the clinic offers the service my pet needs; the tone feels caring rather than intimidating; preventive care is practical and worthwhile; WhatsApp is the easiest next step.
- Proof on hand: public contact details, Instagram presence, physical address in Av. José Joaquín Pérez #3505, Coquimbo, service list, hours, map embed, preventive-plan content, and campaign/coupon flows already present in the site.

## Brand Personality

Warm, responsible, and playful. The brand voice can use affectionate Chilean Spanish such as "peludito" while keeping medical claims clear and grounded. The emotional goal is reassurance: visitors should feel that the clinic is capable, close, and gentle with both pets and their humans.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold hospital sterility, generic corporate pet-care templates, childish mascot overload, unreadable pastel-on-white combinations, and campaign visuals that overpower the clinic's trust signals. The playful space/universe motif should support warmth and memorability without making the medical service feel unserious.

## Design Principles

- Lead with reassurance first: every fold should make booking care feel safe, human, and simple.
- Keep the universe motif purposeful: use cosmic language and details as a distinct brand memory, not as decoration that competes with service clarity.
- Make WhatsApp unmistakable: contact paths should remain visible, specific, and low-effort across pages and devices.
- Balance affection with clinical confidence: friendly copy needs enough precision that guardians trust the care, not just the tone.
- Design for local decisions: address, hours, services, plans, and current campaigns should be immediately scannable for people deciding where to go today.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for public-facing pages. Preserve readable Spanish copy, visible focus states, sufficient contrast for pastel surfaces, touch-friendly tap targets, reduced-motion alternatives for decorative animation, semantic headings, descriptive link text, and useful alternative text for any future real imagery.
